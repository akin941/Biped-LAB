# WP3-03: PPO smoke training on the velocity env

`scripts/train_ppo.py --envs 64 --iters 60 --workers 2 --seed N --require-rise`
uses rsl-rl-lib 5.0.1 with the ADR-0012 settings. The critic gets the 70-dim
privileged group. Each run is 60 iterations = 92 160 samples. Criterion: the mean
per-step reward of the last 15 iterations is above that of the first 15, for all 3
seeds.

| Seed | gha-linux (PR #19, `nightly-train`) | Cloud container |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | −0.0060 → 0.0071, 31 s | −0.0065 → 0.0080, 65 s |
| 1 | −0.0074 → 0.0054, 38 s | −0.0077 → 0.0062, 54 s |
| 2 | −0.0067 → 0.0066, 39 s | −0.0065 → 0.0085, 51 s |

**Criterion met on gha-linux.** Each job took about 1 minute in total, including
the torch install. That is well under the 5-minute budget.

What the rise comes from (cloud seed 0, per-iteration log):
- The action-rate penalty shrinks as the policy's initial std of 1.0 narrows.
- Upright improves slightly.
- Episodes get a little longer (about 22 → 33 steps).

The robot still falls within about 1 s. This is a plumbing check that env, adapter
and PPO learn together. It is not a walking result. Walking needs the full budget
(WP3-04, Mac M5).
